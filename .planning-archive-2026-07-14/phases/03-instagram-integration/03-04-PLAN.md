<objective>
Encode the adaptive top-5 doctor discovery flow (D-09, D-10, D-11) into Pass 1 and Pass 2 prompts so the LLM correctly handles the realistic scenario where a clinic's tituled experts (профессора, КМН) don't have Instagram, but other doctors in the clinic do. The Instagram-active cohort may differ from the site-top-5 cohort — both are legitimate and both belong in the report.

Purpose: Per CONTEXT.md "Specific Ideas" section: "Профессора без Instagram — нормально." The reference report sections 03 (Experts) and 04 (Content Analysis) need real Instagram data, which often comes from doctors #6-#10 on the site, not doctors #1-#5. A naive top-5 selection would miss all Instagram-active doctors. The adaptive rule fixes this.

Output: Augmented prompts in pass_collect.py (doctor discovery flow + adaptive reorder rule) and pass_gap_analyze.py (item evaluation when cohorts differ).
</objective>

## Phase Goal (MVP)

**As a** presale client,
**I want to** see real Instagram metrics for the doctors who actually USE Instagram (not just the tituled professors who don't),
**so that** the Content Analysis section reflects real content strategy insights, not empty placeholders.

## User Story (Vertical Slice)

After this plan, for a cosmetology clinic where:
- Site lists 10 doctors (top-5 are КМН professors without Instagram, doctors #6-#10 are younger cosmetologists active on Instagram)
- find_doctor_handles returns all 10 handles
- run_instagram_content batch returns data for 5 of them (the Instagram-active ones, with `top_by_followers` sorted by follower count)

The report's Content Analysis section (section 04) shows metrics for the top-5 Instagram-active doctors (not the site-top-5). The Experts section (section 03) shows all 10 doctors — tituled ones with their regalia + Instagram-active ones with their metrics.
