# Research Scope: Competitor Analysis for Medical Marketing SEO

**Date:** 2026-05-11  
**Mode:** Deep (8 phases, 10-20 minutes)

## Core Research Question

How to conduct comprehensive competitor analysis for medical marketing SEO that identifies keyword gaps, content opportunities, backlink strategies, and technical advantages to inform strategic decision-making?

---

## Research Components

### 1. Keyword Gap Analysis Methodology (CRITICAL)
- Techniques for identifying keyword gaps between client and competitors
- Prioritization frameworks (difficulty, volume, intent, potential ROI)
- Tools and APIs (SEMrush, Ahrefs, Google Search Console)
- Metrics for opportunity scoring
- Medical marketing specific considerations
- Case studies and success patterns

### 2. Content Strategy Analysis Framework (CRITICAL)
- Methods for analyzing competitor content strategies
- Medical content quality metrics (E-E-A-T, author credentials, citations)
- Content gap identification (topics, formats, depth)
- Publication frequency and update patterns
- Tools for content analysis (BuzzSumo, SEMrush Content Analyzer)
- Medical compliance considerations

### 3. Backlink Profile Analysis Best Practices (CRITICAL)
- Techniques for analyzing competitor backlink profiles
- Donor quality metrics (DR, DA, traffic, relevance, spam score)
- Backlink gap identification and opportunity assessment
- Anchor text distribution analysis
- Link building strategy patterns (guest posts, PR, partnerships)
- APIs and tools (Ahrefs, Majestic, Moz)

### 4. Technical SEO Competitive Audit (CRITICAL)
- Methods for technical competitor audits
- Core Web Vitals benchmarking (LCP, FID, CLS)
- Site structure and internal linking analysis
- Schema markup for medical sites (Organization, MedicalBusiness, FAQPage)
- Mobile optimization comparison
- PageSpeed Insights API and other tools

### 5. Paid Advertising Competitive Intelligence (IMPORTANT)
- Techniques for tracking competitor ad campaigns
- Creative and messaging analysis (USP, offer, CTA)
- Budget estimation methods
- Ad intelligence tools (SEMrush, SpyFu, AdBeat)

### 6. Medical Marketing Compliance (IMPORTANT)
- Medical advertising regulations and standards
- HIPAA compliance for medical websites
- Ethical standards in medical marketing
- Competitor compliance verification

## Key Assumptions to Validate

1. **Assumption:** SEMrush, Ahrefs, and GSC APIs provide sufficient data for comprehensive competitor analysis
   - **Validation:** Research API capabilities, limitations, and pricing

2. **Assumption:** Medical marketing has unique compliance requirements that affect competitor analysis
   - **Validation:** Research medical advertising regulations and HIPAA requirements

3. **Assumption:** Keyword gap analysis is the most critical component for SEO strategy
   - **Validation:** Research industry best practices and expert opinions

4. **Assumption:** E-E-A-T is critical for medical content quality assessment
   - **Validation:** Research Google's medical content guidelines and updates

5. **Assumption:** Core Web Vitals are significant ranking factors for medical sites
   - **Validation:** Research Google's ranking factor studies and medical site performance
