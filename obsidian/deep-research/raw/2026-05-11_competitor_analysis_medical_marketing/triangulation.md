## Contradictions & Uncertainties

### Contradiction 1: Google Analytics HIPAA Compliance

**Source A (HIPAA Guidance):** Google Analytics (any version) is NOT HIPAA compliant - Google won't sign BAA  
**Source B (Some Practitioners):** GA4 with IP anonymization is "HIPAA-friendly"  

**Resolution:** Source A is authoritative (OCR guidance). GA4 without BAA = violation regardless of IP anonymization.

**Confidence:** High - Official regulatory guidance takes precedence

---

### Contradiction 2: Keyword Difficulty Thresholds

**Source A (SEMrush):** KD 0-29 = Easy, 30-49 = Possible, 50-69 = Difficult, 70-100 = Hard  
**Source B (Ahrefs):** KD 0-10 = Easy, 11-30 = Medium, 31-70 = Hard, 71-100 = Super Hard  

**Resolution:** Different tools use different scales. Both valid within their ecosystems. Recommend using same tool consistently for comparisons.

**Confidence:** Medium - Tool-specific metrics, not universal standards

---

### Uncertainty 1: AI Platform Optimization (GEO)

**Source A (Natura Dermatology Case):** 672 AI platform citations, significant traffic source  
**Source B (Limited Research):** GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is emerging field, best practices still developing  

**Status:** Emerging trend with early success data, but methodology not yet standardized

**Confidence:** Medium - Early adopter success, but limited industry consensus
