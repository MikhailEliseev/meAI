## Triangulation Strategy

### Core Claims Requiring 3+ Sources
1. **Keyword gap prioritization formulas** → SEMrush blog, Ahrefs guide, independent SEO expert
2. **E-E-A-T metrics for medical content** → Google guidelines, SEO studies, medical marketing case studies
3. **Backlink quality scoring** → Ahrefs documentation, Moz guide, academic research
4. **Core Web Vitals thresholds** → Google official docs, web.dev, industry benchmarks
5. **Medical advertising regulations** → FDA guidelines, HIPAA docs, legal analysis

### Contradiction Detection Areas
- Tool vendor claims vs. independent reviews
- Google official guidance vs. SEO practitioner experience
- Compliance requirements vs. marketing best practices

### Credibility Assessment Criteria
- **High (80-100):** Official documentation (Google, FDA), peer-reviewed research, established industry leaders
- **Medium (60-79):** Tool vendor blogs, industry publications, expert opinions
- **Low (40-59):** Forum discussions, unverified case studies, promotional content
- **Very Low (<40):** Anonymous sources, outdated content (>3 years), unsubstantiated claims
