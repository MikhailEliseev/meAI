# Research Scope: Keyword Research для медицинского маркетинга

**Date:** 2026-05-11  
**Mode:** Standard (6 phases)  
**Target Audience:** Technical (SEO specialists, medical marketing professionals)

---

## Core Research Question

How to implement effective keyword research for medical marketing in Russia, considering:
- Technical methods and tools (APIs, algorithms)
- Medical domain specificity (terminology, regulations)
- Quality metrics and evaluation frameworks
- Legal compliance (Russian medical advertising law)

---

## Research Components

### 1. Methods & Techniques (CRITICAL)
- Seed keyword expansion strategies
- Long-tail keyword discovery
- Question-based keyword research
- Local modifiers (city, district, metro)
- Medical terminology mapping (lay terms ↔ professional terms ↔ ICD-10)

### 2. Tools & APIs (CRITICAL)
- **Yandex.Wordstat API** — documentation, limits, pricing
- **Google Keyword Planner API** — documentation, limits, pricing
- **Ahrefs API** — capabilities, pricing, alternatives
- **Semrush API** — capabilities, pricing, alternatives
- **SE Ranking API** — capabilities, pricing, alternatives

### 3. Clustering Algorithms (CRITICAL)
- Semantic clustering (meaning-based)
- SERP-based clustering (search results similarity)
- Intent-based clustering (informational/commercial/navigational)
- Automatic vs manual clustering trade-offs

### 4. Quality Metrics (CRITICAL)
- **KEI** (Keyword Effectiveness Index) = (Search Volume)² / Competition
- **Keyword Difficulty** (ranking complexity)
- **Search Intent** classification
- **CPC** (commercial intent indicator)
- **Seasonality** patterns

### 5. Russian Medical Advertising Law (CRITICAL)
- **Federal Law 38-FZ** "On Advertising" (Article 24 on medical advertising)
- **Federal Law 323-FZ** "On Healthcare Fundamentals"
- Prohibited formulations ("100% cure", "guaranteed results", "best clinic")
- License mention requirements
- Penalties for violations

### 6. Competitive Analysis (IMPORTANT)
- Competitor keyword analysis methods
- Gap analysis (missed opportunities)
- Content gap analysis

### 7. Position Monitoring (IMPORTANT)
- Automated position tracking (TopVisor, SE Ranking)
- Update frequency
- Regional grouping

### 8. Analytics Integration (IMPORTANT)
- Yandex.Metrika API (real traffic)
- Google Analytics API (real traffic)
- Keyword-to-conversion mapping

3. **Clustering Guidance** — algorithm comparison with implementation examples
4. **Metrics Framework** — formulas and benchmarks for keyword quality evaluation
5. **Legal Compliance** — specific prohibited terms, requirements, penalties

## Key Assumptions to Validate

1. **Low-frequency assumption** — Medical keywords typically have 10-1,000 searches/month (validate with data)
2. **High-conversion assumption** — Medical keywords convert at 2-5% vs 0.5-1% e-commerce (validate with benchmarks)
3. **Regional dependency** — Local search is critical for medical services (validate with statistics)
4. **API availability** — Yandex.Wordstat and Google Keyword Planner have public APIs (validate documentation)
5. **Legal enforcement** — FZ-38 violations result in significant penalties (validate with case examples)
