<objective>
Redesign the PRESALE conversation flow in Hermes to be a live, step-by-step dialogue with a potential client — not a rigid protocolized report. Hermes asks for the website, does a quick analysis of directions/doctors/revenue, gathers competitors (named or auto-found), evaluates their relevance, runs full CI analysis, and delivers a final report with a friendly conversational summary first, followed by detailed breakdown.

Purpose: Transform Hermes from a "fire all tools and dump a structured report" bot into a natural specialist dialogue — the difference between a machine spitting out data and a friend saying "вот смотри — у тебя тут раз-два-три, а у конкурента такие врачи ключевые, такие темы хайпят."

Output: Updated SOUL.md (PRESALE section), synced _presale_prompt(), and validation test script.
</objective>
