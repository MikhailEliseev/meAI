**As a** presale client in a cosmetology or plastic surgery niche,
**I want to** be sure the LLM is instructed to call Instagram analysis (and Pass 2 is instructed to flag its absence as hard-FAIL),
**so that** the Instagram-critical dimension of my market is not silently skipped at the prompt level.

## User Story (Vertical Slice)

After this plan, for `iphk.ru` (plastic surgery, instagram-critical=true per Plan 03-02 mini-call):
- Pass 1 prompt instructs LLM: "call run_instagram_content after find_doctor_handles" (mandatory language)
- Pass 2 prompt instructs LLM: "if run_instagram_content not called → item 5=missing, HARD FAIL regardless of other items"
- QC checklist item 5 carries `conditional_on_niche: True` flag

- Pass 1 prompt: "Instagram optional"
